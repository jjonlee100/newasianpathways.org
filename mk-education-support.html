Program

MK Education Support

Helping missionary children stay in school so families can keep serving.

In the shadows of persecution, where faith is tested daily, Asian missionaries courageously bring hope to the unreached. Yet, amidst their sacrifices, one of their greatest concerns is their children’s education. How can their children thrive when stability, resources, and security are constantly under threat?

The Challenge

For many missionary families serving in regions hostile to the Gospel, access to quality education is a dream often out of reach. Frequent relocations, financial hardship, and the inability to access local schools due to discrimination or security risks leave missionary children vulnerable to educational neglect. Without proper schooling, their future—and the future of the mission—remains uncertain.

The Solution

By partnering with us, you can provide these children with stable, high-quality Christian education that nurtures both academic excellence and spiritual growth. Through scholarships, online learning platforms, homeschooling support, and safe learning environments, we equip them with the tools they need to flourish—both in their faith and their future careers.

Why It Matters

  • Strengthens the Mission: When missionaries know their children’s education is secure, they can serve with greater focus and longevity.
  • Raises the Next Generation of Leaders: These children grow up with a deep understanding of faith under fire, shaping them into resilient disciples who will carry the Gospel forward.
  • Breaks the Cycle of Hardship: Education empowers them to build a stable future, ensuring that their families can continue in ministry without fear of economic collapse.

Your support can provide

  • Tuition for missionary children
  • Access to safe and high-quality learning resources
  • Emotional and academic counseling for children affected by persecution
  • Technology for remote education

Every gift changes a life. With your generosity, we can turn uncertainty into opportunity for these young hearts.
