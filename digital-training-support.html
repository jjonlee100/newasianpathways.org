Program

Digital Training Support

Learning and counseling that reach leaders anytime—especially where travel is difficult.

As persecution increases and physical access to training decreases, digital platforms have become an essential tool to equip Christian leaders in closed and sensitive regions. Yet the digital divide still leaves many frontline leaders without the training they urgently need.

The Challenge

In persecuted or remote areas, Christian leaders often lack access to theological education, leadership resources, or mentorship. Traditional classroom training is either unsafe or unavailable. Without support, these leaders remain isolated and under-equipped.

The Solution

New Asian Pathways is expanding digital theological training to serve next-generation leaders throughout Asia. By creating new content, translating key resources, and developing robust digital platforms, we ensure that leaders can access strategic training anytime, anywhere from high-quality trainers. With your support, we reach pastors and disciple-makers across barriers.

Why It Matters

  • Breaks Access Barriers: Delivers high-quality theological education in hostile areas.
  • Supports Rapid Multiplication: Trained leaders can reproduce discipleship movements.
  • Leverages Technology: Keeps Gospel training scalable, sustainable, and secure.

Your support can provide

  • Translation and development of digital content
  • Robust and secure digital delivery systems
  • Technical support and platform maintenance
  • Strategic outreach and collaboration with partners
