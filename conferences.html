Program

Conferences & Retreats for Missionaries and Pastors

Rest and renewal for frontline workers so they can return with fresh strength.

Serving on the front lines of the Gospel can lead to spiritual fatigue, trauma, and burnout. Without rest and care, even the most committed leaders may leave ministry prematurely.

The Challenge

Leaders working in persecuted or high-risk regions face daily spiritual and emotional stress, often without access to rest, counseling, or community. Many feel isolated and overwhelmed, and without renewal, their ministries, and health, are at risk.

The Solution

New Asian Pathways hosts regional soul care retreats designed to refresh and restore missionaries and pastors. These safe, visa-free-accessible gatherings offer counseling, biblical teaching, peer fellowship, emotional healing, and much prayer. With your support, we can expand these life-giving retreats across Asia.

Why It Matters

  • Reduces Burnout: Restored leaders stay longer and serve stronger.
  • Multiplies Impact: Leaders return renewed, better equipped to lead others.
  • Builds a Resilient Network: Regional retreats foster deep connection and collaboration.

Your support can provide

  • Travel and lodging for participants
  • Facilitation of soul care and counseling
  • Materials, meals, and retreat logistics
