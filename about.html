About us

Equipping indigenous leaders to sustain the local church in restricted regions

New Asian Pathways comes alongside national leaders already serving on the ground—with training, tools, and backing for the long haul.

Who we are

Support behind the scenes

In many parts of Asia, local Christian leaders and pastors faithfully serve where living out their faith is difficult or dangerous. They already know the language, the culture, and how to navigate their own communities best.

New Asian Pathways (NAP) exists to support these national leaders behind the scenes. Rather than sending external teams to take over or start brand-new programs, NAP comes alongside local leaders who are already doing the work—providing encouragement, training, and practical resources so they can thrive.

Why our work is critical

The “China barrier”—and what it taught us

For decades, traditional missions relied on sending foreign workers into places like China. Due to shifting political climates, strict government restrictions, and high security risks, almost all foreign workers have been forced out or compelled to leave.

Because of the severe risks involved, traditional organizations often cannot send people back. But local, indigenous leaders never left. They have been faithfully carrying on the ministry that has existed for generations.

Where foreign workers face closed doors and high barriers, NAP partners directly with local believers who are already on the ground. We don’t try to force foreign presence into places where it puts people at risk; instead, we support the local leaders who are already there—doing the work safely and effectively.

Our approach

Sustaining established ministry

When foreign workers were forced to leave, they didn’t leave a void—they entrusted the work to capable local leaders. The ministry didn’t end; it was handed off to those best equipped to carry it forward.

Carrying the torch

Local partners have taken full ownership of the ministry, ensuring that decades of Gospel work continue seamlessly within their own communities.

Walking alongside, not taking over

Instead of restarting or replacing what was left behind, NAP acts as a supportive partner—providing encouragement, guidance, and backing as national leaders expand their reach.

A lasting model

By equipping entrusted national leaders rather than relying on outside presence, the ministry remains resilient, adaptable, and self-sustaining through any future challenge.

Why NAP is unique

Partnership that lasts

We don’t reinvent the wheel

Local leaders have been doing this work long before us. We come alongside to strengthen and support what is already happening—not take it over.

We empower local ownership & sending

National leaders aren’t just maintaining ministry—they are actively raising up new leaders and sending out their own teams across Asia and around the world.

We build genuine relationships

Our partnerships are anchored in personal commitment, trust, and shared calling—not one-off transactions.

The bottom line

A support system for the long haul

NAP serves as a vital support system for frontline leaders in restricted areas across Asia. By coming alongside indigenous partners with training, tools, and backing, we help local ministries stay resilient, strong, and equipped for the long haul.
