Program

Family, Children & Youth Camps

Camps that help young people and families grow in faith—even under pressure.

In Asia’s most challenging mission fields, youth and families are not only surviving under pressure—they are rising to lead. Camps provide them with life-changing encounters with Christ, strong peer support, and leadership development.

The Challenge

Young people growing up in persecuted or unreached areas often face isolation, fear, and limited access to Christian community. Without investment, they are vulnerable to hopelessness or cultural assimilation that draws them away from faith.

The Solution

Our youth and family camps offer a vital response—safe, faith-building environments where young believers can grow spiritually, discover their callings, and form Gospel-centered friendships. We also empower families and future leaders through coaching and teaching during these events.

Why It Matters

  • Builds Gospel Identity: Camps nurture faith in young believers surrounded by cultural or religious pressure.
  • Inspires Leadership: Many attendees return as leaders, mentors, and church planters.
  • Strengthens Family Units: Families are discipled together, equipping them to stand strong in faith.

Your support can provide

  • Full life-changing camp experiences for 250 youth
  • Registration and lodging for families
  • A full-time camp director to sustain momentum and growth from a nearby country
