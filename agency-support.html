Program

Agency Support

Standing with local sending agencies—and the workers they care for—through every storm.

Missionaries serving in hostile or unreached regions face more than ministry challenges—they face medical crises, government threats, trauma, and natural disasters. Without a reliable support system, these challenges can force families off the field.

The Challenge

Local sending agencies often lack the resources to offer consistent care, insurance, crisis response, or logistical support to their missionaries. This puts frontline workers at serious risk and can severely limit long-term mission effectiveness.

The Solution

New Asian Pathways provides essential agency support services that sustain missionaries for the long haul. We offer strategic member care, insurance support, and crisis response funding to help local leaders and their families stay healthy, safe, and focused on the mission.

Why It Matters

  • Prevents Avoidable Loss: Missionaries are protected when unexpected crises strike.
  • Increases Longevity: With holistic care, missionaries remain longer with greater impact.
  • Strengthens Indigenous Agencies: Empowers local networks to send and support well.

Your support can provide

  • Emergency medical insurance and care
  • Crisis response and evacuation funds
  • Strategic member care visits and consultations
  • Relief for disaster-struck ministry communities
