From Difficult Places to Difficult Places

Across Asia, national pastors and Christian leaders already know the language, culture, and communities they serve, often in places where faith is difficult or dangerous. We come alongside them behind the scenes with encouragement, training, and practical resources.

Who we are

Walking alongside, not taking over

New Asian Pathways (NAP) exists to support national leaders who are already doing the work. Rather than sending external teams to take over or start brand-new programs, we provide encouragement, training, and practical resources so local ministries can thrive.

When foreign workers face closed doors, local indigenous leaders never left. They carry ministry that has existed for generations. Where outsiders put people at risk, we partner directly with believers already on the ground, safely and effectively.

Read our story

Why it matters

Sustaining Established Ministry

When foreign workers left restricted regions, they did not leave a void, they entrusted the work to local leaders best equipped to carry it forward.

Carrying the Torch

Local partners have taken full ownership so decades of Gospel work continue within their own communities.

Walking Alongside

We do not restart or replace what was left behind. We back national leaders as they expand their reach.

A Lasting Model

By equipping entrusted national leaders, not relying on outside presence, ministry stays resilient and self-sustaining.

Why NAP is unique

Strengthen What Already Exists

We Don’t Reinvent the Wheel

Local leaders have been doing this work long before us. We strengthen and support what is already happening, not take it over.

Local Ownership & Sending

National leaders raise up new workers and send their own teams across Asia and around the world.

Genuine Relationships

Partnerships are built on trust, personal commitment, and shared calling, not one-off transactions.
