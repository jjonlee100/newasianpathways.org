Our Mission

About New Asian Pathways

New Asian Pathways seeks to journey with the persecuted church in Asia and its agencies to fulfill their unique call in the Great Commission and especially encouraging it when unique challenges seem insurmountable.

Our members have been dedicated to supporting the persecuted church in Asia for many years. We partner with Christian national leaders to overcome unique challenges and support evangelism, discipleship, church planting, leadership development, and sending missionaries to Unreached People Groups (UPGs) both within and outside of their country.

Given the current political climate, direct missionary engagement in many countries has become increasingly difficult. Our emphasis is non-residential support, mentoring, and capacity-building for national leaders—while encouraging short-term sending and long-term multicultural teaming.

What We Do

Ministry Distinctives

New Asian Pathways equips and supports leaders, families, and youth to advance the Gospel across Asia, especially in hard-to-reach and persecuted regions.

MK Education Support

Scholarships and resources so missionary children can thrive while families remain in ministry.

Cross-Cultural Training

Centers that equip indigenous leaders to engage Animist, Muslim, Tibetan, and other UPGs.

Indigenous Agency Support

Member care, insurance, crisis response, and partnership with local sending agencies.

Soul Care Retreats

Rest and renewal for house church leaders and missionaries in visa-accessible settings.

Youth & Family Ministry

Camps and discipleship that raise resilient believers and equip families under pressure.

Digital Training & Micro-Business

Online theological training and sustainable livelihoods for church planters.

Our Gift Commitment

Building Gospel Partnerships Through Relational Trust

In our mission context—where partnership is not only strategic but sacred—we believe gift agreements should reflect mutual calling, shared vision, and long-term relational commitment.

~90¢ of every donated dollar goes directly to ministry

We strive to keep overhead as low as possible and build agreements anchored in transparency, mutual accountability, Kingdom alignment, and flexibility—echoing Paul’s description of the Macedonian churches giving “first to the Lord, and then by the will of God to us” (2 Cor 8:5).
