From Difficult Places to Difficult Places

National pastors and local Christian leaders already know the language, culture, and communities they serve, often in places where faith is difficult or dangerous. We come alongside them behind the scenes with encouragement, training, and practical resources.

Who we are

Journey alongside. Strengthen what local leaders began.

New Asian Pathways comes alongside indigenous leaders who are already doing the work. We do not send teams to replace them. We offer encouragement, training, and practical help so local churches can endure in restricted regions.
